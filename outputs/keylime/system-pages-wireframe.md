# KeyLime Marketing — System Pages Wireframe

Applies to `/systems/foundation`, `/systems/growth`, and `/systems/expansion`.

```text
HEADER
HERO: system name · price · outcome statement · [Book Free Consult]
CALCULATOR NUDGE: “Not sure? See what missed calls cost first →”
WHO IT FITS: persona-led, two-column text panel
EVERYTHING INCLUDED: grouped capability checklist with service anchors
ADD-ONS / CUSTOM WORK: contextual callout + /contact route
ONBOARDING: 4–8 week, three-stage timeline
SYSTEM FAQ: 3–5 native details accordions
FINAL CTA: Calendly + View full comparison
FOOTER
```

Growth receives the single featured treatment: accent label and slightly elevated hero/card. Expansion adds the multi-location callout. On mobile all sections stack; the calculator nudge remains immediately after the hero.
