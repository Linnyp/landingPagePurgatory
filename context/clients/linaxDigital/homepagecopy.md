- [x] Average sentence length under 20 words
- [x] Contractions throughout
- [x] Opens with concrete things — verbs, scenes, real questions
- [x] Each section ends on an idea or calm invitation, not "Contact us today!"

- [x] No fabricated stats or testimonials
