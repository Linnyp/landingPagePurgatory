## Rationale on the choices

- **Hero is locked.** Kept the spec'd H1 / sub / button verbatim — it's the calculator pattern-break and the loss frame the docs are explicit about. Calculator is embedded above the fold per arch v3.0 (the hero *is* the calculator entry, not a teaser pointing to one). Two alternates provided for later A/B testing only.
- **"Tier" is dead — everything's a "system."** Per arch v3.0 rename. Updated in section headers, FAQ answers, How It Works step 1, button labels, and the URLs (`/tiers/*` → `/systems/*`).
- **CTA standardized as "Book Free Consult."** Final CTA right card and any header/footer CTA references use the locked label from arch §0.4 and §6.5. "Book a free call" has been retired.
- **Trust bar now carries inline social proof.** Industries row + named-business logo strip (Four Leaf Charters, Verona Cabinets, Mycelia Foundation, Mega Kovas, ord-x, Virtue Sod) — substitutes for the deferred `/case-studies` (P4) per arch §0.3.
- **Industries Snapshot expanded into "Built for home services and beauty."** Per arch §5.1, the homepage absorbs vertical framing that `/industries/*` will own in P3. Mike + Lena persona snapshots come straight from `personas.md`. Industry toggle re-runs the embedded hero calculator with vertical-tuned defaults.
- **New: Pricing transparency callout (§10).** Explicitly called out in arch §5.1 between testimonials and FAQ. Defuses the "what does this actually cost" objection before the FAQ stack.
- **Differentiator block kept as a bridge.** Arch §5.1 doesn't carve a standalone slot, but the messaging is too load-bearing to lose. If design pushes back at launch, fold the H1 into the closing of How It Works and the body into the Three Systems section header.
- **No HighLevel, no A2P 10DLC, no AI-powered.** Compliance language translates to "SMS compliance handled" and "the law requires." Platform stays unnamed throughout.
- **No unverified stats.** Three guarantees stand in for proof on launch. A "verified outcomes coming soon" line acknowledges the gap without faking it. Testimonial slot is empty by design — fill with verified pulls only.
- **No citrus puns.** None anywhere. Confirmed.
- **One idea per section.** Each section advances exactly one argument before handing off to the next.
- **CTAs are action-specific.** "Calculate my missed-call revenue," "Book Free Consult," "Talk to us about a custom quote" — never "Get started," "Learn more," or "Submit."
