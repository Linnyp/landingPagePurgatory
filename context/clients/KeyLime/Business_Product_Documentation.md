**KeyLime Marketing**

*Business & Product Documentation — v4.0*

# **1\. Company Overview**

## **1.1 About KeyLime Marketing**

KeyLime Marketing is a digital marketing company that helps local service businesses generate more leads, win more jobs, and keep more customers — without forcing the owner to learn a stack of disconnected software. We package modern marketing into a managed system: one team, one platform, one monthly price. Owners run their businesses; we run their marketing.

KeyLime is built around a three-tier system model — Foundation, Growth, and Expansion — that combines done-for-you platform features (CRM, missed-call text-back, review generation, booking, follow-up, and more) with specialized work (SEO, paid ads, custom websites) when a business is ready for it. Each tier is a complete, working system the day it goes live, designed to be added to as the business grows.

Founded in Southwest Florida, KeyLime is positioned to serve local service businesses nationwide. Our initial sales and onboarding push focuses on Southwest Florida — where we have direct market knowledge, customer reference points, and the ability to meet operators in person — but the system itself is built to be deployed in any market a local service business serves.

## **1.2 Mission Statement**

To put a real, working marketing system in the hands of every local service business — no jargon, no software stack to assemble, no learning curve. We do the work; the business gets the results.

## **1.3 Who We Serve**

KeyLime serves local service businesses, with a primary focus on two industries:

* **Home services.** Remodeling, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, garage doors, epoxy and flooring, pest control, landscaping, pressure washing, pool service, and similar trades.

* **Beauty and personal services.** Salons, barbershops, med spas, lash and brow studios, nail salons, massage and body work, and similar appointment-based personal services.

These two industries share the operational shape KeyLime is built for: repeat customer relationships, appointment-driven revenue, reliance on local reputation, and a strong need for fast lead response. Our ideal client is an established or growing operator who knows their trade but doesn't have the time, team, or interest to manage a marketing stack themselves.

While early sales effort is concentrated in Southwest Florida (Lee, Collier, and Charlotte counties), our systems are not geography-locked. Any qualifying local service business in the U.S. can be onboarded onto the same platform, with the same tier structure, at the same price.

## **1.4 Business Model**

KeyLime operates a service-and-system hybrid model. Revenue comes from three sources:

1. **Monthly system subscriptions.** Foundation, Growth, and Expansion tiers, billed monthly. Each tier is a bundle of platform solutions and (in Growth and Expansion) specialized work, priced as a package well below the cost of the underlying solutions purchased individually.

2. **Onboarding fees.** A one-time $200 setup fee per engagement, covering account provisioning, initial integrations, and the gameplan deliverable.

3. **Add-on solutions and specialized work.** Any solution not included in a client's chosen tier can be added on. Some add-ons have published pricing; others — particularly specialized work like SEO programs, paid ads management, and custom website builds — are scoped and quoted based on the specific business.

The model is deliberately a hybrid rather than pure agency or pure SaaS. Replicable platform features powered by HighLevel form the backbone of every account, so we're not rebuilding marketing infrastructure from scratch for every client. On top of that backbone, we layer specialized work where it produces outsized returns: a well-built website, real SEO, and well-managed paid ads. The mix means we can serve a $99/mo new business and a $495+/mo growing operator with the same underlying system.

We believe in fair, value-based pricing. Our tier prices are published openly. Where pricing depends meaningfully on scope — a custom website, an SEO program for a competitive market, a managed ad budget — we quote rather than guess. The goal is to be clear about what things cost without burying prospects in a hundred line items.

## **1.5 Brand Voice**

KeyLime speaks to business owners the way a useful, plain-spoken neighbor would. We explain what we do in terms of the problems it solves — missed calls, slow follow-up, no reviews, an empty calendar — not in terms of features or acronyms. We don't lead with technology. We lead with results. The everyman archetype guides all customer-facing communication: confident without being arrogant, expert without being condescending, modern without being cold.

# **2\. The KeyLime Platform**

## **2.1 What the Platform Is**

Every KeyLime client is provisioned a dedicated sub-account on our HighLevel-powered platform. The platform is the system we use to deliver our services — it's not a SaaS product the client buys, learns, and operates. Clients get access to use the parts they need (the CRM, the unified inbox, reporting), while KeyLime configures and runs the automations, workflows, and integrations behind the scenes.

This is the core of the service/SaaS hybrid: the platform's heavy lifting is replicable across clients in the same industry, so we can deploy a working system in days, not months. Configuration and minor customization tailor it to each business; what doesn't need to be customized stays standardized, which is what keeps our pricing accessible.

## **2.2 Included With Every Account**

Every KeyLime account, regardless of tier, includes the foundational platform infrastructure needed for SMS, voice, and compliant outbound communication:

* Dedicated Twilio business phone number

* Full A2P 10DLC registration and compliance (brand registration, campaign approval, ongoing carrier compliance)

* HighLevel sub-account with full configuration

* Standardized industry workflows for the client's vertical

* KeyLime team monitoring and management

A2P 10DLC compliance is a hard requirement for legitimate SMS marketing in the U.S. and is handled as part of onboarding so clients never have to think about it.

## **2.3 Platform Solutions (the 12)**

The following twelve solutions are delivered through the platform. Different solutions are included in different tiers; any solution not included in a client's tier can be added on.

1. **Lead Pipeline (CRM).** Every lead, contact, and customer tracked in one place. Pipeline stages reflect how the client's business actually moves work from inquiry to closed job or booked appointment.

2. **Speed-to-Lead.** The package that makes sure no new lead falls through the cracks. Missed-call text-back (every missed call instantly responds with a text), auto-response and routing on form submissions, and form pre-qualifications that filter and tag leads before they reach the owner.

3. **Lead Nurture.** Multi-touchpoint follow-up sequences across SMS and email. Cold leads, quote follow-ups, no-show recovery, and long-cycle decision support — automated, on time, every time.

4. **Reputation Management.** Automated review requests after job completion or appointment, review monitoring across Google and Facebook, sentiment-aware response handling, and ongoing optimization of the review flow.

5. **Rewards.** Customer loyalty and referral mechanics built into the platform. Points, rewards, referral tracking, and the campaigns that drive participation.

6. **Birthday Campaigns.** Automated birthday outreach with offers tailored to the business — especially powerful in the beauty industry, also effective for home service repeat customer retention.

7. **Chatbots.** Website and social chat with industry-tuned conversation flows. Captures leads, answers common questions, books appointments, and routes anything complex to a human.

8. **Booking.** Online appointment booking integrated with the CRM and the business's calendar. Branded booking page, customer self-service rescheduling, automated confirmations.

9. **Unified Inbox.** SMS, email, webchat, social DMs, and Google Business messages — all in one inbox. The team replies in one place; the platform handles the routing.

10. **Appointment Reminders.** Automated multi-channel reminders to reduce no-shows: confirmation on booking, reminder a day out, reminder the morning of, and follow-up after.

11. **Voice Agents.** AI voice agents that pick up calls after hours, capture lead information, and book appointments directly into the calendar. For businesses that lose meaningful revenue to missed evening or weekend calls.

12. **Database Reactivation.** Targeted campaigns to past customers and dormant leads — the easiest revenue most businesses leave on the table. Run on a quarterly cadence in the Expansion tier; available as a one-time or ongoing campaign on any tier.

## **2.4 Specialized Solutions (the 3)**

Beyond the platform solutions, KeyLime delivers three specialized services that go beyond what the platform alone can do:

* **SEO.** From a foundation SEO build (citations, keyword mapping, GBP optimization, technical fundamentals) all the way through ongoing content programs, link building, and competitive market work.

* **Google & Meta Ads.** End-to-end paid ads delivery — strategy, copy, creative, audience setup, campaign management, ongoing optimization, and reporting. Google Search, Performance Max, Local Services Ads, and Meta (Facebook/Instagram).

* **Websites.** From included template-builder sites in lower tiers to full custom Webflow builds in the Expansion tier and beyond. Mobile-first, conversion-focused, integrated with the platform.

Specialized solutions are partially included in Growth and Expansion tiers and available as add-ons to any tier when a business is ready for them. Pricing depends on scope and is quoted per engagement.

# **3\. The Three-Tier System**

## **3.1 Overview**

KeyLime's core offering is a managed digital marketing system delivered at one of three tiers. Each tier is a complete, working system from day one. The tiers are designed to fit different stages of business maturity, marketing budget, and growth ambition — and any tier can be extended with add-on solutions when a need shows up.

* **Foundation — $99/month.** The minimum viable marketing system. For new businesses, businesses on a tight budget, or established operators who want to test the platform before committing to more.

* **Growth — $195/month.** The full lead-generation engine. For established businesses ready to invest in consistently generating, capturing, and converting leads.

* **Expansion — $495/month.** The full customer lifecycle system. For growing businesses that want a real digital footprint — custom website, content marketing, full lifecycle automation — in addition to the lead engine.

All three tiers include the $200 one-time setup fee and the 4–8 week onboarding process.

## **3.2 Setup Fee & Onboarding**

**Setup fee: $200 (one-time).** Covers platform provisioning, A2P 10DLC registration, baseline configuration, and the gameplan deliverable.

**Full onboarding: 4–8 weeks.** The depth of onboarding is what makes the system actually fit the business. It covers:

* **Business documentation.** Discovery sessions to gather the information needed to configure the platform around the business's specific operations, services, pricing, target customers, and team structure.

* **Platform account setup.** HighLevel sub-account provisioning, Twilio number assignment, A2P 10DLC registration and approval, integrations with the client's existing systems (booking software, payments, calendars, lead sources).

* **Strategy blueprint (gameplan).** A written marketing gameplan tailored to the business — the channels, automations, content, and campaigns that will run, and the priority order in which they'll be implemented.

* **Workflow build.** Configuration of every included solution: pipelines, automations, message templates, review flows, follow-up sequences, booking pages, and reporting dashboards.

By the end of onboarding, the system is live, the team is trained on the parts they touch, and the gameplan is in motion.

## **3.3 Foundation — $99/month**

**Who it's for.** New businesses, very low-budget operators, or established businesses that already have a website and want to start with a core system before committing to more. Foundation is also the right starting point for owners who want to see how KeyLime works before scaling up.

**What's included.**

* Lead Pipeline (CRM)

**The idea.** Foundation gives a business the three things every operator needs immediately: a place to put leads, a system that drives reviews, and a single inbox that consolidates customer messages. It's a complete working system, intentionally lean, ready to expand when the business is.

Most home service and beauty operators see Growth pay for itself in the first month from missed-call recovery alone.

It's the tier where digital marketing stops being a line item and starts being a meaningful driver of growth.

## **3.6 Add-On Solutions**

Any solution not included in a client's chosen tier can be added on. Add-ons fall into two pricing categories:

**Published add-on pricing.** Platform solutions that are not in a client's tier — Lead Nurture (on Foundation/Growth), Voice Agents, Rewards (on Foundation/Growth), Database Reactivation as one-time campaigns, GMB management on lower tiers, additional automations and chatbot builds — are available at published add-on prices. Clients add what they need; the monthly stays clear and itemized.

**Custom-quoted specialized work.** SEO programs beyond the foundation, ongoing content production beyond what's bundled, Google and Meta ads management, custom website projects, and large bespoke workflow builds are quoted per engagement. Pricing depends meaningfully on the scope, the competitive market, the ad budget, or the complexity of the build — and rather than publish placeholder numbers that don't reflect reality, we quote based on the actual work.

The principle: clients should always know what something costs before they buy it. They shouldn't have to wade through dozens of line items to figure out what their plan covers.

## **3.7 How Tiers Move**

Tiers are not locked in. A Foundation client who outgrows the lean system can move to Growth; a Growth client who's ready for content marketing and a real website can move to Expansion. Tier upgrades preserve everything that's already configured — the same sub-account, the same contacts, the same workflows — and add the new included solutions on top.

The same is true in the other direction: a client can drop tiers if business circumstances change. The system is structured to grow and contract with the business, not to lock the owner into something that no longer fits.

# **4\. How the System Grows With the Business**

KeyLime is designed around the idea that the right marketing system at year one is not the right marketing system at year three. The three-tier structure plus add-on solutions creates a deliberate growth path:

* **Step 1 — Start at the right tier.** Foundation, Growth, or Expansion, based on where the business is today. The system is live and producing value within 4–8 weeks.

* **Step 2 — Add solutions as needs surface.** As the business grows, gaps appear: a busy operator needs Voice Agents to capture after-hours leads; a salon needs Rewards to retain regulars; a contractor needs Database Reactivation to mine old quotes. Each is added on without renegotiating the entire engagement.

* **Step 3 — Layer in specialized work.** When the system is producing leads but the business wants more of them, paid ads come in. When organic search becomes a strategic priority, SEO scales up. When the website becomes the bottleneck, a custom build replaces the template.

* **Step 4 — Move tiers when it makes sense.** A Foundation client at $99/mo and a long list of add-ons may be better served by moving to Growth. A Growth client adding custom website and content may make more sense on Expansion. We surface the math; the client decides.

* **Step 5 — Consolidate.** Over time, the client replaces a patchwork of disconnected tools — separate CRM, separate review tool, separate booking software, separate texting platform, separate email system — with one managed system. The lifetime relationship deepens; the operational drag on the owner disappears.

# **5\. Technology & Infrastructure**

## **5.1 Platform Stack**

The KeyLime platform is built on HighLevel's white-label infrastructure. KeyLime operates the parent agency account; each client engagement lives in an isolated sub-account. This architecture keeps client data segmented while letting KeyLime standardize workflows, automations, and reporting across the client base — the standardization is what makes the service/SaaS hybrid economics work.

Telephony and SMS infrastructure runs through Twilio, with full A2P 10DLC registration handled per client to maintain carrier compliance for outbound SMS.

## **5.2 Specialized Work Stack**

* **Web development:** Webflow (Expansion tier custom builds and standalone custom website projects); HighLevel's native site builder for Foundation and Growth tiers.

* **SEO tooling:** Ahrefs, Google Search Console, Google Analytics, Google Business Profile.

* **Ads:** Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager.

* **Design:** Figma with Figma-to-code pipelines.

* **AI-assisted workflows:** Claude and custom multi-agent systems for research, content drafting, competitor analysis, and operational tooling.

## **5.3 Delivery Model**

Day-to-day delivery is handled by a combination of in-house team capacity and vetted specialist contractors brought in for design, content, link building, ad operations, and custom development work. As the business scales, contractor-supported delivery transitions to dedicated in-house roles, then to specialized service-line teams — without changing the client-facing experience.

# **6\. Client Onboarding**

## **6.1 The Onboarding Process**

Onboarding runs 4–8 weeks depending on tier and the complexity of the business. The structure is consistent across tiers; the depth and scope adjust to the engagement.

* **Week 1 — Discovery and gathering.** Discovery sessions, credential and access collection (website, hosting, GBP, ad accounts, analytics, registrars, booking software, payment processors), and the initial business documentation buildout.

* **Weeks 2–3 — Platform setup.** HighLevel sub-account provisioning, Twilio number assignment, A2P 10DLC registration submission, integrations with the client's existing systems, baseline workflow configuration.

* **Weeks 3–5 — Gameplan and build.** The written strategy gameplan is delivered. Workflows for every included solution are built and tested. Where specialized work is included (Webflow site, SEO foundation, GBP optimization), production begins.

* **Weeks 5–8 — Activation and training.** Final QA on every workflow, A2P 10DLC approval received, team training on the parts of the platform they'll use directly, and system activation. From this point the system is live and the ongoing relationship begins.

## **6.2 Single Point of Contact**

Every client is assigned an account lead who is the single point of contact for the engagement. The account lead coordinates the underlying production work — internal team, contractors, specialists — so the client doesn't have to chase five different people for an answer.

# **7\. Competitive Positioning**

KeyLime competes on five differentiators:

* **A complete system, not a checklist of services.** Most local marketing buyers are asked to assemble a stack: SEO from one vendor, ads from another, a CRM from a third, a review tool from a fourth, a website from a fifth. KeyLime delivers one managed system at one monthly price.

* **Service/SaaS hybrid economics.** Replicable platform features mean we can offer real marketing infrastructure at prices that make sense for a $300K-revenue business, not just a $5M one. Lower-tier clients get a real working system, not a stripped-down demo.

* **Industry focus.** Home services and beauty are the verticals our platform configurations are tuned for. Industry-specific workflows, language, integrations, and benchmarks are built in — not adapted from a generic template.

* **Plain-English communication.** We explain what we do in terms of the problems we solve. Owners don't need to learn what A2P 10DLC is or how attribution modeling works. They need their phones to ring more, their no-shows to drop, and their reviews to keep coming in.

* **Built for growth, both directions.** The same client can start at Foundation for $99/mo, expand to Expansion with added paid ads at $1,500+/mo, and contract back down if business conditions change — all on the same platform, with the same team, without rebuilding.
